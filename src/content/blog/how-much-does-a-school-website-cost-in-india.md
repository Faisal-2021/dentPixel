## Why pricing varies so much

School website pricing in India is like asking "how much does a car cost?" — a Maruti Alto and a Toyota Fortuner both get you from A to B, but the experience, reliability, and inspection readiness are very different.

The biggest price drivers are:

1. **CBSE compliance requirements** — Bye-Law 8.10 mandatory disclosure sections are non-negotiable for CBSE-affiliated schools and add structured work
2. **Online admission system** — UPI integration, document upload, application tracking adds 2–5x engineering effort vs static pages
3. **Custom CMS vs WordPress template** — A school-specific dashboard vs a generic blog admin panel
4. **City tier** — Agencies in Mumbai charge 2–3x what agencies in tier-2 cities charge for identical work
5. **Inclusions** — Photography, content writing, logo design, email setup, SMS gateway are almost always extra unless explicitly listed

Let's break every single component down.

## 2. Feature-by-Feature Cost Breakdown

This is where 90% of the budget variation happens. Each feature below is a discrete module with real engineering, design, and compliance work behind it. Pick only what you need.

### 2.1 CBSE Bye-Law 8.10 Mandatory Disclosure (14 sections)

The single most requested add-on. CBSE Bye-Law 8.10, updated in 2025, requires every affiliated school to publish 14 specific sections on their official website with PDF documents that match SARAS portal records.

What's included:

- School management committee details & minutes
- Fee structure (class-wise, bus-wise, term-wise)
- Staff qualification details with appointment letters
- Affiliation letter, extension, and NOC copies
- RTE admissions quota details & records
- Academic calendar, syllabus, and term dates
- PTM schedule and grievance redressal mechanism
- Safe drinking water, sanitation, and fire safety certificates
- Student-teacher ratio and section-wise strength
- Academic results (board exam pass percentages)

**Cost: ₹8,000 – ₹25,000 (one-time)**, plus ₹2,000–₹5,000/year for document updates as records change. Our [CBSE school websites](/cbse-school-websites) package includes this module pre-built with quarterly audits.

> [!WARNING]
> "We already have a disclosure page" is not enough. In 2025, 340+ schools across Delhi, Haryana, and UP received show-cause notices because their disclosure PDFs were outdated or links were broken. DIOS/DEO inspectors **click every link** during affiliation renewal.

### 2.2 Online Admission Forms with UPI Integration

The admissions season in India runs April–June for most schools, February–March for FYJC in Delhi/Mumbai. A manual admissions process (collect forms at the counter, cash payment, manual entry into registers) costs a school 15–20 hours of office staff time *per day* during peak season.

A modern online admission module includes:

- Customizable application form with class/stream selection
- Document upload (birth certificate, Aadhaar, TC, marksheets)
- Application fee payment via UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm), net banking, or card
- Application tracking dashboard for parents (application ID → received → verified → accepted)
- Admin panel with export to Excel, SMS/WhatsApp updates to applicants
- Merit list / draw-of-lots auto-generation for oversubscribed RTE quotas

**Cost: ₹15,000 – ₹50,000 (one-time)**, plus ₹2,000–₹5,000/year for payment gateway compliance and form updates. See our dedicated [online admission website](/online-admission-website) service for what this looks like end-to-end.

Payment gateway setup fees are **₹5,000 – ₹15,000** (Razorpay, PayU, CC Avenue) and transaction fees range 1.5–2% per transaction. For a school collecting ₹50 lakh in admission fees, that's ₹75,000–₹1,00,000 in gateway fees — but the alternative is cash handling risk, queues, and forged receipts.

### 2.3 Custom School CMS & Admin Dashboard

This is the difference between "we need to call the developer every time a circular goes out" and "the principal can post from her phone over morning chai."

A school-specific CMS includes:

- News & events publishing (scheduled posts, photo galleries, PDF attachments)
- Circulars & notices board with SMS/WhatsApp alert integration
- Results publishing (class-wise, student-wise roll number lookup)
- Gallery & album management (photo compression for mobile, privacy modes)
- User roles (Principal, Admin, Teacher, Accountant — each with different access)
- One-click backup to Google Drive

WordPress can be jury-rigged for this, and many "cheap" developers go that route. But it breaks often. A purpose-built school CMS avoids plugin conflicts, speed issues, and the "we got hacked again because we didn't update 12 plugins" panic that hits schools every July.

**Cost: ₹10,000 – ₹30,000 (one-time)**, plus ₹3,000–₹8,000/year in maintenance.

- **General agency:** Only if you have a very specific, bespoke requirement (custom alumni donation platform, multi-language campus map with live bus tracking, integrated learning management). Expect to pay premium rates and manage the project actively.

## 6. Seven Hidden Costs to Avoid in 2026

These are the items that appear as "surprise invoices" after the build. Every single one should be in the original quote.

### 1. Content writing

A 10-page school website needs roughly 8,000–12,000 words of original copy — about the school's vision, pedagogy, faculty bios, individual class descriptions, and admission process. Most quotes include "copy-paste from your existing site" but not original writing. Professional education copywriters charge **₹12,000–₹30,000** for a full site.

### 2. Professional photography & stock images

A school lives and dies by its visuals. Parents on WhatsApp share screenshots of the campus gallery more than any other page. Professional school photography (half-day shoot, 300+ edited photos, students in uniform, classrooms, labs, playground) costs **₹15,000–₹40,000** in tier-1 cities, ₹8,000–₹20,000 in tier-2. Budget at least ₹5,000 even for a licensed stock image package — unlicensed Google Image photos get schools slapped with copyright takedown notices.

### 3. Logo design & branding refresh

"Can you just touch up our logo while you're at it?" If the quote doesn't list it, the answer is: "That's a separate branding project, ₹10,000–₹35,000."

### 4. Email account setup & migration

Google Workspace for Education Fundamentals is free for eligible schools, but the setup (DKIM, SPF, DMARC records, migrating old Gmail/Outlook emails, training staff) takes 4–8 hours and costs **₹4,000–₹12,000**. Most quotes skip this.

### 5. SSL certificate renewals

Free Let's Encrypt SSL certificates expire every 90 days. If your host doesn't auto-renew (and cheap shared hosting doesn't), you need to budget **₹0 for the cert + ₹1,000–₹3,000 for renewal labor every quarter** — or just go with a host that includes auto-renewal.

### 6. Migration from old site

If you have an existing school website on another platform, migrating content (pages, old news, gallery photos, PDF circulars) to the new site takes 4–12 hours and costs **₹5,000–₹15,000**. Schools routinely forget this and then complain "but all our old news is gone!"

### 7. Redesigning again in 12 months

The single biggest hidden cost: buying a cheap site now and then paying for a full redesign in a year because it failed CBSE inspection or didn't bring in admissions. A ₹20,000 site + ₹50,000 redesign in Year 2 = ₹70,000 total, when a proper ₹45,000 site from day one would have cost less and been stable for 4–5 years.
