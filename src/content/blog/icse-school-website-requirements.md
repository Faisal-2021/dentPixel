If you're searching for "ICSE school website requirements" or "ICSE mandatory disclosure," start here: **CISCE does not have a direct equivalent to CBSE's Mandatory Public Disclosure rule.**

Most administrators land on this page because they apply the familiar CBSE compliance framework to an ICSE school. The two boards work differently. CBSE prescribes exactly what must appear on your website and where. CISCE — the ICSE/ISC board — combines affiliation prerequisites, inspection cycles, and the RTE Act rather than a single web-disclosure checklist.

This post covers what an ICSE-affiliated school actually needs on its website and where the requirements come from. No fabricated clauses, no invented penalties — just the factual framework.

> [!INFO]
> **TL;DR.** CISCE does not mandate a "Mandatory Public Disclosure" page in the CBSE sense. Your obligations come from three sources: (1) CISCE Affiliation Bye-Laws prerequisites, (2) the RTE Act, 2009 and state rules, and (3) CISCE inspection expectations. The checklist at the end covers the minimum.

## ICSE vs CBSE: Where the Confusion Comes From

The search term "ICSE mandatory disclosure" is common and a category error. Here is why the confusion persists.

CBSE wrote its disclosure format into its affiliation bye-laws. Every CBSE school publishes a fixed 14-category disclosure page that inspectors check during every renewal, and regional offices now run automated desk audits. For principals moving between boards, it is natural to assume ICSE works the same way.

CISCE took a different route. Instead of a web template, its compliance model relies on:

- **Documentary prerequisites** at affiliation time
- **Periodic on-site inspections**
- **Annual academic and financial reporting**
- **RTE-mandated transparency** applicable to all private schools

Your ICSE website does not need a labelled "Mandatory Public Disclosure" page in the CBSE format. It does need substantial information public — and regional offices increasingly expect it on the website rather than in a cabinet.

## What CISCE Affiliation Actually Requires

Both ISC affiliation bye-laws and the broader CISCE affiliation requirements set the same institutional prerequisites for ICSE schools. While not framed explicitly as website disclosures, every item below should either appear on your website or be producible from it on request.

### Legal and institutional prerequisites

- **Registered governing entity.** School run by a registered Society, Trust, or Section 8 Company with education as the primary object. Publish entity name, registration number, and registering authority.
- **Governing Body and Managing Committee.** Names, designations, and term dates per CISCE-prescribed composition.
- **State Government NOC.** Valid NOC from the state or UT administration for provisional and permanent affiliation.
- **Land documentation.** Certificate of Land establishing ownership or long-term lease of the required campus area.
- **RTE recognition.** Recognition certificate from the state authority under the RTE Act, 2009.

> [!WARNING]
> **Avoid the "we'll show it during inspection" trap.** Inspectors now increasingly expect to find these documents on the website first. Downloadable PDFs let inspectors complete a desk review before visiting, shortening the on-site audit.

### Academic and financial requirements

- **English medium of instruction** stated clearly on the Academics or About page
- **ICSE/ISC curriculum statement** with subject streams offered
- **Published academic calendar and exam schedule** updated each session
- **Fee structure** for the current academic year, itemized
- **Admission schedule** and refund policy (financial stability evidence)

## RTE Disclosure Rules That Apply to ICSE Schools

This is the most often missed piece. **The RTE Act, 2009 applies to nearly all private schools — including ICSE-affiliated ones — regardless of board.** Section 19 and state-specific RTE rules form the bulk of your actual public disclosure obligations.

Implementation varies by state (your state education department's RTE rules are authoritative), but these categories are required nearly everywhere:

- **Complete fee structure.** Tuition and all charges, itemized, current-year. [NEEDS VERIFICATION: Some states cap fee increases or require fee regulatory committee approval — confirm your state's rules.]
- **Admission process and eligibility.** Criteria, age limits, documentation, timeline, intake per class.
- **EWS / Disadvantaged Group quota.** Seats reserved, procedure, and current admitted list. [NEEDS VERIFICATION: Reservation percentage varies by state — typically 25% at entry level per RTE Section 12(1)(c), but confirm.]
- **Infrastructure details.** Classrooms, labs, library, playground, drinking water, separate toilets, ramps, medical room.
- **Teaching staff details.** Names, designations, qualifications — minimum by department with qualifications.
- **Academic calendar** and exam schedule.
- **SMC composition** where applicable under state RTE rules.

> [!TIP]
> **Overlap is significant.** If you already publish RTE disclosures you are roughly 70–80% of what CISCE inspectors expect. The remainder is CISCE-specific documentation (NOC, land, society/trust) and board-specific academic info.

## CISCE Inspection and Annual Reporting

The third pillar of ICSE school compliance. Anything inspectors ask to see during a visit should be findable online first — reduces friction and signals institutional discipline.

CISCE monitors affiliated schools through:

1. **Periodic inspections.** Scheduled, covering standards, infrastructure, governance, and conditions. [NEEDS VERIFICATION: Exact frequency varies — provisional schools inspected more often.]
2. **Annual academic returns.** Enrolment, staff, results, attendance.
3. **Annual financial returns.** Income-expenditure, fee collections, development fund utilisation.
4. **Grievance resolution.** CISCE parent/public complaint channels; inability to produce documentation weakens position on merits alone.

Returns go to CISCE directly, but summaries (results, staff, fees) are what parents and panels will look for on your site first.

## ICSE (CISCE) vs CBSE: Side-by-Side Comparison

| Requirement Area | ICSE / CISCE | CBSE |
|---|---|---|
| Named "Mandatory Public Disclosure" rule | No equivalent bye-law. Obligations from RTE + inspection expectations + prerequisites. | Yes — codified with a prescribed 14-category format. |
| Primary enforcement | On-site inspections, annual returns, RTE panels. | Desk audits of website first, then inspections. |
| Fee structure publication | Required under RTE state rules (varies by state); CISCE expects transparency. | Explicitly required, dated and current. |
| Staff qualification publication | Required under RTE (detail state-dependent). | Explicitly required: individual names, qualifications, particulars. |
| Affiliation certificate + NOC | Producible; best-practice on website. | Required as attested downloadable PDFs. |
| Building / fire / safety certificates | Inspected on-site; recommended upload. | Required as attested PDFs. |
| Management committee details | CISCE requires proper constitution; publish names terms. | Explicitly required as part of MPD. |
| Result statistics last 3 years | Submitted in returns; publication recommended. | Explicitly required in the format. |

> [!SUCCESS]
> **Key takeaway.** If you build to a reasonable CBSE MPD standard you'll exceed CISCE expectations. Just don't label the page "CBSE Mandatory Public Disclosure" for an ICSE school — use "School Information & Disclosures" or "Governance & Transparency" instead.

## What a Compliant ICSE School Website Should Include

The working ICSE website checklist we use for every site we build. It is calibrated for CISCE inspections, RTE requirements, and parent inquiries.

### 1. School Identity and Affiliation

- Full school name, address, phone, email, office hours
- Affiliation statement: "Affiliated to CISCE, New Delhi"
- Affiliation code (if issued) and status (Provisional / Permanent)
- Registered Society / Trust / Section 8 Company name and registration number
- Principal name with qualifications

### 2. Governance

- Governing Body / Managing Committee members names, designations, terms
- SMC composition where RTE-applicable

### 3. Statutory Documents (PDFs)

- State Government NOC, RTE recognition, land/ownership certificate, building safety, fire safety, sanitation certificates

> [!INFO]
> **Date every PDF upload.** Add document issuance date and "Last reviewed: [date]". Undated certificates are treated as unverified even if legible.

### 4. Academics

- English medium of instruction statement
- ICSE/ISC curriculum with streams/subjects offered
- Current academic calendar with exam dates and term breaks
- Last 3 years ICSE/ISC result summaries: pass %, appeared, distinctions

### 5. Admissions and Fees

- Admission policy, eligibility, age limits, intake per class
- Current year fee structure itemized
- Fee refund policy
- EWS quota details

### 6. Infrastructure

- Campus area, classroom/lab counts and sizes, library, playground, water, toilets (girls/boys), ramps, medical room
- Actual (not stock) photos of each facility

### 7. Staff Directory

- Teaching staff by dept with names and qualifications
- Key admin, special educators, counsellors
- Pupil-teacher ratio

## Common ICSE School Website Gaps

From audits across multiple states these five gaps repeat most often:

1. **No fee structure published.** The single most frequent RTE parent complaint. Publish the fee structure if you publish nothing else.
2. **Governing body list outdated.** A Managing Committee showing 2022 members in a 2026 inspection signals neglect.
3. **Statutory documents offline only.** NOC and land certificates on the website save inspectors lookup time and avoid flagged items.
4. **Result statistics stale.** Class X/XII results are the #1 admission-season page; outdated data raises parent suspicion.
5. **EWS quota information missing.** RTE Section 12(1)(c) applies nearly everywhere; a missing EWS page invites complaints.

## Building Your ICSE Website: 7-Day Timeline

You don't need a six-month redesign. The compliance layer is structurally simple — documents, tables, clearly-named pages:

- **Days 1–3: Gather.** NOC, RTE recognition, land/ownership, safety certificates, current fee structure, last 3 years results, academic calendar, committee/staff lists, facility photos.
- **Days 4–6: Structure.** Single nav-accessible section (e.g., `/transparency`) using the 7 checklist categories. Date every PDF, add "Last updated" under every table.
- **Day 7: Cross-link & test.** Link from footer, About, Admissions. Click every PDF, verify mobile rendering, add "Disclosures verified: [date]."
